# Evidence and limits

This is a bounded public summary of the September 10 contribution catalog and dated private review material. Except for the two linked public pull requests, underlying tests, packages and acceptance records are withheld and are not independently inspectable in this bundle. No historical test counts are added together.

## Slide 1

The September 10 contribution catalog reports two merged public fixes and sixteen privately accepted packages. Those states have different meanings: a public merge is inspectable upstream; private component acceptance is summarized here without its underlying test artifacts. The sixteen-package count belongs to September 10 and is not a current total. The full program remained incomplete in that snapshot.

## Slide 2

Public evidence: [Distinct-signer counting, PR 65](https://github.com/oma3dao/mpas/pull/65) and [strict signed-approval parsing, PR 68](https://github.com/oma3dao/mpas/pull/68). Both pages record merge on August 28, 2026, and describe regression tests. They are separate changes. Neither merge establishes a later release, production deployment or acceptance of the private package program. Distinct identities do not prove independent control.

## Slide 3

Private evidence summary from the September 10 catalog and its strict-input and canonical-verifier reviews: reject ambiguous input; validate trusted application and execution rules; bind approvals to the intended payload, authorized key and time window; reject conflicting signer decisions. These are plain repair summaries. No private source patch, exploit recipe or current-upstream defect claim is disclosed.

## Slide 4

Private evidence summary from the corrected policy package: preserve all/any rule meaning across the policy engine and response consumers; compute remaining requirements; apply proposer exclusion before satisfiability; reject malformed policies. Earlier boundary defects and their corrections remain part of the retained private review history. Component acceptance is distinct from final integrated acceptance.

## Slide 5

Private evidence summary from the signer/authority package: define discovery and review behavior, bind the displayed request to the actual action, validate the material before submission, and distinguish review metadata from provider permissions. A specification or interoperability change is not proof that live agents comply with it.

## Slide 6

Private evidence summary from the result-disclosure package: enforce a reviewed result policy across requests and recovery, keep denied results local, preserve receipts with allowed results, and prevent caller mutation from silently changing permission. The retained source summary says historical stored bytes remain intact while new disclosure is filtered. No underlying implementation is supplied here.

## Slide 7

Private evidence summary from the receipt package: verify the signature, issuer authority, action/payload binding, outcome and time before returning trusted receipt data. A verified receipt remains the issuer’s report and does not prove the external effect occurred as reported.

## Slide 8

Private evidence summary from policy recovery, coordination and durable dispatch work: distinguish temporary policy unavailability, approval progress, delivery and actual execution; retain dispatch and result state; preserve indeterminate outcomes without automatic retransmission. Scoped recovery and process-crash checks do not establish physical power-loss durability or universal exactly-once effects.

## Slide 9

Private evidence summary from login/session and transport protection work: bind sessions to the intended account and request, check permission before credential use, and prevent implicit retry or permission expansion from producing an extra target action. Controlled local providers and synthetic credentials were used. This is not live-provider compatibility evidence.

## Slide 10

Private evidence summary from bridge/generator work: check registry, plugin identity and input; bound schema-processing work; preserve capability errors; sign direct requests by default. A trusted direct-only exception was narrowly configured and could not be enabled by request data. The detailed private patch and tests are withheld.

This slide preserves that earlier snapshot; it is not current migration status. Later September 11 private source acceptance summarized in the retained September 17 work report supersedes the partial state for its exact combined candidate. No live account connection is established by either statement.

## Slide 12

A dry run is not a release, and the historical component result is not final integrated conformance.
