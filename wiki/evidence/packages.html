Packages

Contribution purposes without double counting or patch instructions

A package is a review and delivery unit. Several packages can change the same behavior, and a combined candidate can contain earlier repairs. The retained package descriptions below do not add finding credit or authorize applying a patch. Their exact private source maps and dependency records are withheld.1

The application package's earlier partial scope is historical. The separate September 11 combined-source record reports a full offline migration of 24 plugins, 29 authority positions and 52 references, within synthetic-provider limits. This does not establish live deployment.2
