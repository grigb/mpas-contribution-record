Receipts

Dated findings, mechanisms and evidence limits for making receipts independently checkable

An execution receipt is a signed report of an outcome. The receipt repair adds a verification interface that requires trusted action and payload inputs and the authority of the expected issuer. It checks structure, signed bytes, outcome, action binding, time, and the issuer's relationship to the signing key. Failed verification returns no trusted payload. Receipt creation also binds the stated issuer to the key actually used.1

This makes it possible for another component to reject a forged, misbound, or unauthorized receipt. It does not establish that the external service performed every claimed effect, and it adds no retry or execution behavior. Those limits are part of the contribution, not exceptions to hide.1
